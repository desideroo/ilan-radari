The role The Maintenance Planning Engineer is responsible for developing and optimizing preventive maintenance strategies, leading planning & scheduling efforts, and improving equipment reliability to support sustainable production. This role bridges engineering excellence with operational needs, ensuring maintenance activities are executed effectively, cost-efficiently, and in alignment with safety and quality standards. What you'll be responsible for Create, manage, and optimize preventive and predictive maintenance plans. Develop daily, weekly, and shutdown maintenance schedules with cross-functional alignment. Analyze equipment breakdowns and conduct root cause analyses (RCA). Track and control maintenance KPIs (MTTR, MTBF, OEE) and continuously drive improvement. Collaborate with production, engineering, and third-party service providers to ensure high uptime. Manage spare part inventory planning and ensure SAP/CMMS systems are kept up to date. Support TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) initiatives, particularly under PM Pillar. Lead or participate in maintenance-related cost-saving and digitalization projects. Ensure maintenance compliance with safety, quality, and environmental standards. SKILLS: Maintenance Expertise: Preventive & predictive maintenance, root cause analysis (RCA), reliability improvement, maintenance cost control Digital Tools: Hands-on knowledge of SAP PM, CMMS tools (e.g. Boysweb, DFOS), Excel-based reporting and KPI monitoring TPM & Lean Understanding: Good understanding of TPM (especially PM Pillar), 5S, Kaizen, and structured problem solving Planning & Prioritization: Strong capability in workload planning, resource allocation, and proactive issue management Analytical Thinking: Ability to interpret technical data, downtime trends, and maintenance performance metrics Communication & Collaboration: Skilled in working with cross-functional teams (production, quality, external suppliers) and reporting to management Leadership & Ownership: Takes initiative, follows up with discipline, and can lead small improvement projects with limited supervision Adaptability: Comfortable working in dynamic factory environments with varying levels of ambiguity We're looking for someone who Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Industrial Engineering, or a related field. Working knowledge in FMCG/Manufacturing environments is a strong plus. 1–3 years of experience in maintenance planning, budgeting, and optimization. Solid management, leadership, decision making and influential skills and experience is critical Familiarity with root cause analysis (RCA) and continuous improvement methodologies. Hands-on experience with CMMS systems (e.g. SAP PM, Boysweb, DFOS). Strong communication, collaboration, and influencing skills. Demonstrated ability to analyze data, make decisions, and lead improvement initiatives.Proven track record of implementing improvement ideas. Must have in-depth working knowledge and experience in maintenance optimization and cost saving. Solid understanding of TPM, particularly PM Pillar and Lean tools is desirable. Fluent in Turkish & English required.
